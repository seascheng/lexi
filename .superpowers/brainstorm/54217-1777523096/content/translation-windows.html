Translation Display Modes

Three configurable modes for showing translation results

Mode 1: Always-on Bar (常驻顶栏)
⌘⇧T
ephemeral
短暂的，转瞬即逝的 · adjective · lasting for a very short time
↑ Fixed at screen top, width ~70%, centered, semi-transparent background
Mode 2: Auto-hide Bar (自动收起)
serendipity
意外发现美好事物的能力 · noun · the occurrence of events by chance in a happy way
↑ Slides in from top after ⌘⇧T, auto-hides after 5s idle
Mode 3: Popup Card (弹窗卡片)
ubiquitous
✕
无处不在的，普遍的
adjective · present, appearing, or found everywhere
"Smartphones have become ubiquitous in modern society."
↑ Appears near cursor, click outside or Esc to close
